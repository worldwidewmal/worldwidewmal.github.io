## Pre-Outreach Research Checklist

- What makes it premium vs. standard (equipment, guide quality, exclusivity, access)
- What their content underplays (environment, build-up, arrival, contrast between calm and peak)
- The strongest content angle (preparation → peak moment arc, not just highlight reel)
- What you would specifically shoot
