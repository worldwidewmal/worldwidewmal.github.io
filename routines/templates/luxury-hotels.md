- What the property actually offers (room types, amenities, unique spaces)
- What makes it different (design, exclusivity, location, service level)
- What their current content underplays (guest POV, arrival, emotional tone, detail)
- What angle would make them stand out more clearly
- What you would specifically shoot (a concrete visual sequence)
