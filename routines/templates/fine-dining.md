# Template: Fine Dining

Vertical: Fine dining restaurants — elevated, experiential, occasion-driven

## Key Angle
The strongest concept is NOT "look at the food." It is pace, atmosphere, service, plating, and the feeling of the full dining experience. Content should make the viewer feel what it is like to dine there, not just see what is served.

## Pre-Outreach Research Checklist

Before drafting, confirm:
- Signature dishes or tasting menu structure
- Plating style and visual aesthetic
- Room design and lighting
- Chef presence or tableside moments
- Wine or cocktail program
- Whether current content shows food only or the full experience

## Best Content Angles for Fine Dining

Pick one of these and make it specific to the restaurant:
- The full dining progression (arrival → seating → first pour → courses → dessert)
- Chef and service rhythm
- The room and atmosphere between courses
- A signature tasting sequence
- A date-night or occasion angle
- Luxury detail from arrival to final course
